Next Gen Sequencing & Variant Calling

Explore advanced Next-Gen Sequencing (NGS) workflows and comprehensive variant calling analysis for precise genomic insights.

Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) has revolutionized genomics, enabling rapid and cost-effective sequencing of entire genomes and transcriptomes. It encompasses various technologies, including:

  • Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS): Comprehensive analysis of an organism\'s entire DNA sequence.
  • Whole Exome Sequencing (WES): Focuses on the protein-coding regions of the genome, which are most likely to contain disease-causing variants.
  • RNA Sequencing (RNA-Seq): Quantifies and profiles RNA to study gene expression, alternative splicing, and novel transcripts.
  • ChIP-Seq: Identifies DNA binding sites of proteins, providing insights into gene regulation.

Variant calling is a critical step in NGS data analysis, identifying genetic variations such as Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms (SNPs), insertions, and deletions (indels). This process typically involves alignment of reads to a reference genome, followed by sophisticated algorithms to detect and filter variants with high accuracy.

MOCO\'s NGS & Variant Calling suite offers robust tools for quality control, alignment, variant detection, annotation, and visualization, streamlining your genomic research from raw data to actionable biological insights.
